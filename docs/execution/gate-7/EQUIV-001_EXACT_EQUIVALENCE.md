# EQUIV-001 — Cross-provider Exact Equivalence

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | `HARD GATE IMPLEMENTED — NO APPROVED GROUPS` |
| Current cross-provider Exact | `DISABLED` |
| Approval | Named human reviewer and dated immutable evidence required |

Matching model or marketing names do not prove equivalence. An approved group must contain at least two immutable route versions and evidence for:

- identical canonical family/version;
- identical semantic contract hash;
- compatible prompt/reference/first/last/audio semantics;
- resolution, duration and output contract;
- safety, privacy and retention contract;
- capability fixtures and quality evaluation.

Every member carries capability and quality evidence hashes. Differing semantic hashes reject the entire group. Exact failover requires both source and destination route versions in one group; otherwise the Engine keeps the original route, fails before acceptance or requires an explicitly different product mode.

No local KIE/OpenRouter group is registered because no real cross-provider capability and quality evidence exists.
